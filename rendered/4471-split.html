Where each problem stands

Engineering update on #4471, closed 5 October:

  • parser: fixed. fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  • hint: won’t fix. The booking-v3 form is retired, so no change is planned. The hint was never corrected.
  • one-day: open. The update does not mention it, and neither fix addresses it.

What each problem is

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, so a day-first tenant’s dates swap day and month. It reaches every channel.
  • hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, but the form reads the tenant’s way. People follow the hint and the date swaps. Only form reports from day-first booking-v3 tenants belong here. The symptom matches parser, and the fix is in the form.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day with no swap, which month-first parsing cannot do. The cause is not confirmed. The Lisbon and Toronto report points to time zones; check that first.

How the count was made

  2. Place each report by its tenant, form and channel with causes.ts. Do not use the intake tag: it is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and it disagrees with the tenant often.
  3. Move the reports on the desk’s one-day list out of the parser count, because their text does not describe a swap.

The #4471 table still files one-day reports under parser by tenant, so its parser row is larger than the one here.
